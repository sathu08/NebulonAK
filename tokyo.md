# 3-Day Tokyo Trip Plan

## Day 1: Traditional Tokyo
- **Morning**: Visit Senso-ji Temple in Asakusa
  - Explore Nakamise-dori street for traditional snacks and souvenirs
- **Afternoon**: Visit Ueno Park and Tokyo National Museum
- **Evening**: Explore Akihabara for electronics and anime culture
  - Dinner at a local ramen shop

## Day 2: Modern Tokyo
- **Morning**: Visit Shibuya Crossing and Hachiko Statue
- **Afternoon**: Explore Harajuku Takeshita Street for fashion and street food
- **Evening**: Visit Tokyo Skytree for panoramic city views
  - Dinner in the Ginza district

## Day 3: Nature and Culture
- **Morning**: Visit Meiji Shrine in Yoyogi Park
- **Afternoon**: Explore Tsukiji Outer Market for fresh sushi
- **Evening**: Relax at Odaiba waterfront
  - Farewell dinner with traditional Japanese cuisine

## Practical Information
- **Transportation**: Get a Suica or Pasmo card for easy travel on trains and buses
- **Accommodation**: Consider staying near major train stations for convenience
- **Language**: Download translation app; basic Japanese phrases are helpful
- **Currency**: Japanese Yen (cash is preferred at smaller establishments)

## Recommended Packing List
- Comfortable walking shoes
- Weather-appropriate clothing (check season)
- Portable charger
- Reusable water bottle
- Small umbrella (weather can change quickly)
